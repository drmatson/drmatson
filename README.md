# Margarita Matson

⚡ Energy Informatics Researcher (TalTech) • 📊 Data Science (10+ years) • 🏠 Smart buildings & HEMS • 🧠 Forecasting • 🧮 Optimization & MPC

I’m a researcher at **Tallinn University of Technology (TalTech)** working at the intersection of **energy data science, forecasting, and control/optimization**.  
My focus is on turning real-world energy data into **actionable methods** for smarter, more flexible, and more efficient buildings.

---

## What I work on 🔬
- 🏠 **Home Energy Management Systems (HEMS)** and building energy analytics  
- 📈 **Short-term load forecasting** and feature engineering (calendar / weather / behavioral signals)  
- 🧮 **Optimization & control** (e.g., Model Predictive Control, tariff-aware scheduling)  
- ⚡ **Flexibility & demand response**: baselines, verification, and operational constraints  
- 🧰 **Reproducible energy data science**: clean pipelines, metrics, and evaluation protocols
